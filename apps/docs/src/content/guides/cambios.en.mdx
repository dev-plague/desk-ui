## Version 0.2.0 — October 22, 2026

<Badge variant="info" className="my-3">v0.2.0 · Published</Badge>

### Added

- **Page Builder** at `#/constructor`: 20 blocks, layer tree, six templates,
  live theme, responsive preview, JSX/JSON export and `localStorage` saving.
- Iconic applications: `FileExplorer`, `Wizard`, `PropertiesDialog`, `Notepad`,
  `Terminal`, `BlueScreen`, `Screensaver` and `Minesweeper`.
- `Desktop`, `StartMenu` and `WindowManager` with drag, resize, focus and z-index.
- Retro store and social network showcases.
- `Panel`, `Card`, `Separator` and `Toolbar` containers.
- **Spanish/English i18n** for the site and component built-in strings, with
  `DeskUILocaleProvider`, an ES/EN switcher and `?lang=en`.
- **Agent skills** in `skills/`, `AGENTS.md`, `llms.txt` and copying from
  `deskui init`.
- Search with `Ctrl+K`, per-route metadata, MDX content and code-splitting.
- **Publication**: registry served by the site at `/r`, license audit, release
  check and a migration guide between versions.

### Changed

- The product is now **DeskUI**, with the `deskui` CLI, `@deskui/*` packages, the
  `deskui.css` template and the `classic` base theme. The gradient theme becomes
  `gradient`.
- The registry grows from 33 to **49 items** and includes `i18n` as a dependency
  of components that use built-in strings.
- The builder uses the full available height and the site hides the sidebar on
  that route.
- `ScrollArea` draws its own classic scrollbar with arrows and dragging.
- The site loads catalog, laboratory, guides and builder on separate routes.

### Fixed

- The dialog keeps focus inside even when the browser returns it to the document.
- Escape closes the search even when the input contains text.
- MDX guides no longer nest paragraphs inside `AlertDescription`.
- The builder `ProgressBar` and the templates use headings in order.

### Migrating from 0.1.0

The rename and the theme identifiers affect projects that copied components with
the previous version. See the migration guide to compare files, keep your edits
and pin the right registry.

The full history lives in `CHANGELOG.md`, at the repository root.
